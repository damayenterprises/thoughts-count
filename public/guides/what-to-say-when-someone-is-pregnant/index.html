What to say when someone tells you they are pregnant

Someone just shared one of the biggest pieces of news a person can share, and how you respond becomes part of how they remember the moment.

The good news is that this is simpler than it feels. Match their energy, make it about them, and let your warmth do the work.

What matters most

The most meaningful thing you can do is match their joy and follow their lead, because not every pregnancy is met with the same certainty, and your job is to meet them where they actually are.

What to say

“This is wonderful news. I am so happy for you.”
Clear, warm, and impossible to misread.
“How are you feeling about it all?”
Opens the door without assuming how they feel.
“I am so glad you told me.”
Honors the trust it took to share.
“You are going to be so loved by this little one.”
Celebrates them, not just the pregnancy.

What to avoid

“Was this planned?”
Turns a happy moment into an interrogation.
“You are going to be huge, just wait.”
Comments on their body they did not ask for.
“Let me tell you about my delivery.”
Hijacks their news with a story or a warning.

Small ways to show up well

Let them set the pace

Some people want to talk for an hour, others just wanted you to know. Follow their cue.

Send a note a few days later

A warm message after the initial rush tells them the joy stuck with you.

Ask what kind of support they want

Meals, company, or just someone who does not make it a big production.

Keep their news theirs

Do not share it onward until you know they are ready for you to.

What to say depending on the situation

A close friend or sibling
Skip the polish and be real. Tell them exactly how excited you are, then ask how they are actually doing underneath the excitement.
A coworker
Keep it warm and brief. A simple 'congratulations, that is lovely news' respects the moment without prying into anything personal.
A first-time parent
They may be equal parts thrilled and nervous. Celebrate first, then reassure them that they do not have to have it all figured out yet.
Someone who took a long or hard road to this
Let the weight of it show. 'I know how much this means to you' says you have not forgotten what it took to get here.
When you are not sure how they feel about it
Lead with a neutral, caring opener like 'how are you feeling about it?' and let their answer tell you whether to celebrate loudly or simply be present.

How to keep showing up

Check in a few weeks later with a simple 'how are you doing?' that is about them as a person, not a status update on the pregnancy.

Common questions

What do you say when someone tells you they are pregnant?

Lead with genuine happiness and make it about them, something like 'this is wonderful news, I am so happy for you,' then ask how they are feeling rather than jumping to questions.

What should you not say to a pregnant person?

Avoid intrusive questions like whether it was planned, comments about their body or weight, and unsolicited advice or scary birth stories. None of it is yours to bring up.

What if I am not sure they are happy about the pregnancy?

Start neutral and caring. Ask how they are feeling about it and let their answer guide you, so you can celebrate loudly or simply be a steady presence.

How do I respond to a coworker who is pregnant?

Keep it warm and brief. A sincere 'congratulations, that is lovely news' is perfect, and you can leave anything more personal for them to raise.

Is it okay to ask questions when someone shares they are pregnant?

Open-ended and caring questions are welcome, like 'how are you feeling?' Avoid the prying kind about planning, timing, or their body.

What is a thoughtful thing to do after someone tells me they are pregnant?

Send a warm note a few days later and offer specific, low-pressure support, and keep their news private until they are ready to share it more widely.
