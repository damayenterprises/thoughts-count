What to write in a farewell card for a coworker

When a coworker moves on, whether to a new job, retirement, or a fresh chapter somewhere else, a farewell card can end up as a wall of hurried signatures. A couple of real lines stand out and stay with them.

Here is what to write, what to leave off, and a few ways to send them off that go past the card itself.

What matters most

The lines people remember name something specific: a project you got through together, the way they treated the team, a small kindness. Warm and particular always beats a polished blank.

What to say

“It has been a genuine pleasure working alongside you, and the place will feel different without you.”
Naming the gap you will feel is honest and kind.
“Thank you for always making time to help, even on your busiest days. I learned a lot from you.”
A specific thank-you shows you paid attention.
“Wishing you every good thing in this next chapter. They are lucky to have you.”
A forward-looking wish fits a fresh start.
“I will miss our morning check-ins more than the coffee.”
A small shared detail proves the note came from you.

What to avoid

“Don't forget us little people when you're famous!”
A joke about being forgotten can land as insecure.
“Lucky you, getting out of this place.”
Complaints about work sour their send-off.
Just your name and nothing more
On a farewell card it reads like you barely noticed they were leaving.

Ways to send them off well

Say it out loud too

A few honest words in person land harder than any card.

Send the longer note after

A message once they have settled in reminds them the bond outlasts the desk.

Offer to stay in touch, and mean it

Trading a real way to reach you turns a goodbye into a keep in touch.

Mark the send-off

Lunch or a small gathering gives the team a proper way to close the chapter.

What to write depending on your relationship

A close work friend
Be personal and a little tender. Name what you will actually miss and make plans to stay in each other's lives.
A boss or manager who is leaving
Thank them for something specific they taught you or made possible, and keep it warm but professional.
Someone on your team you didn't know well
Keep it short and sincere. A genuine good luck in your next chapter is plenty.
Signing a group card when you barely knew them
One warm line is fine. Wishing you all the best beats crowding in a joke that isn't yours to make.

How to keep showing up

The first weeks in a new place can feel lonely. A short message a month later, just checking how the new chapter is treating them, tells them the friendship did not end at the door.

Common questions

What do you write in a farewell card for a coworker?

Something specific and warm: thank them for a way they helped, name what you will miss, and wish them well in their next chapter.

What is a short goodbye message for a coworker leaving?

Try: It has been a real pleasure working with you. Wishing you all the best in what comes next.

What should you not write in a coworker's farewell card?

Skip complaints about work, jokes about being forgotten, and leaving only your signature.

What do you write for a boss or manager who is leaving?

Thank them for something they taught you or made possible, and keep it warm but professional.

What do you write when you barely know the coworker?

Keep it short and sincere. A simple good luck in your next chapter is genuine and enough.

Is it okay to be funny in a farewell card?

A light, kind joke works if you know them well. When in doubt, warm and sincere is the safer choice.
